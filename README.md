## 1. Credit Card Fraud Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of credit card transactions to identify fraud patterns, calculate key metrics with SQL, and present the findings in an interactive Tableau dashboard.

![Credit Card Fraud Dashboard](Fraud-Analysis/Screenshot%202026-10-24%20000613.png)

**Highlights**
- KPIs: total transactions, fraud cases, fraud rate, fraud amount
- Fraud cases and amounts by category
- Monthly fraud trends
- Fraud vs. non-fraud comparison with interactive filters

**Tools:** MySQL, Tableau

[View project folder](Fraud-Analysis)

## 2. Telco Customer Churn Analysis

An analysis of customer churn in a telecom company to understand why customers leave and which factors influence churn, presented through a dashboard.

**Tools:** Power BI
